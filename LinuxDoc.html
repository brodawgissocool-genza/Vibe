What is a LinuxOS?

a **LinuxOS** is an open-source operating system kernel originally developed by Linus Torvalds. It is completely customizable, which is why developers love it and why it is a major inspiration for the architecture of Vibe.

how to install

Unlike AndroidOS, **LinuxOS** is completely installable on almost any computer. You can download different versions called "distributions" (such as Ubuntu, Debian, or Arch) and flash them directly onto a flash drive to install them.
